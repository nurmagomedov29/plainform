Plainform builds Android applications for people who use them on a job, not at a desk. Each one does a single thing precisely, works without a signal, and shows how it arrived at its answer.

Applications

Cable Size Calculator

Conductor sizing to NEC, BS 7671 and AS/NZS 3000, with ambient and grouping derating, voltage drop, and every step of the working shown. For electricians, estimators and inspectors.

Android · in preparation

How these are built

  • Offline first. The calculation never touches the network. A plant room with no signal is the normal case, not the edge case.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics. There is nothing to log in to and nothing collected about you.
  • The working is shown. A number you cannot check is a number you cannot defend to an inspector.
  • It refuses rather than guesses. Where a standard's table runs out, the app says so instead of inventing a value.
