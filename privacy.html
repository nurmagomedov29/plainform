Privacy Policy — Cable Size Calculator

Applies to the Android application dev.cablecalc. Last updated 5 October 2026.

The short version

The calculator runs entirely on your phone. The load current, run length, voltage, insulation, installation method, ambient temperature and grouping you enter are used to compute a result on the device and are then discarded. They are never transmitted anywhere, never stored on a server, and never seen by Plainform. The app works with the radio switched off.

The app shows advertising, and the advertising is the only part that touches the network. What that involves is set out below in full.

What the app itself collects

Nothing leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no cloud storage. Plainform holds no information about you at all.

Some information is stored on the device, because the app would be useless without it: saved jobs, and — if you buy the Pro unlock — the company name, job reference and logo image that are printed on the worksheet. That material is written to the app's own private storage, readable by no other app, and it is included in a PDF only at the moment you choose to export or share one. It is never uploaded. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app erases all of it, permanently and with no copy anywhere else.

Photos and the company logo

Choosing a logo opens Android's own system photo picker. The app requests no storage permission and has no ability to read your photo library; the picker hands back the single image you selected and nothing else. That one image is copied into the app's private storage, resized, and the original location is not retained. It is used for one purpose — printing your logo on your own worksheets — and is not transmitted anywhere.

Payment

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase, never a subscription. It is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Plainform never sees your payment details, card number, address or Google account; the app receives only a signed token saying that this device's Play account owns the product. Your purchase history is governed by Google's privacy policy.

What the advertising collects

Advertising is served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google receives information from your device in order to select and measure that ad. Google acts as an independent controller of this data.

DataWhy
Advertising IDTo select ads and limit how often the same ad is shown. You can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
IP address, and the approximate location derived from itTo serve ads relevant to your country or region, and to comply with regional law.
Device model, OS version, screen size, languageTo render the ad correctly.
Ad interactions (impressions, taps)To measure and bill advertising.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob policies.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before any ad request is made, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your choice is recorded on your device and honoured on every later launch. If you decline personalised advertising, ads are either non-personalised or absent, and the calculator continues to work in full. Nothing in this app is withheld on the basis of that answer.

You can change or withdraw that choice at any time. Where a consent form was shown to you, the app also shows a Privacy settings control at the foot of the main screen, below the calculator; tapping it reopens the same form, and your new choice governs every ad request the app makes from that point on. An advert already on screen stays until it refreshes; nothing new is requested against the old answer. No data of yours is affected — your saved circuits, company name and logo are untouched.

Outside those regions the control is not shown, because no consent form was presented and there is no recorded choice to revisit. What is available to everyone, in every region, is the advertising identifier itself: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and Google's record against your device goes with it.

Children

This is a tool for electrical trades and is not directed at children. It is not listed in the Play Store's Families programme, and no part of it is designed to appeal to children.

Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. To remove the advertising identifier Google holds against your device, reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or uninstall the app.

Accuracy, and what this app is not

Cable Size Calculator is a planning aid. It applies published tables and derating factors to the figures you enter. It cannot know your installation, and it is not a substitute for the current edition of the applicable standard or for the judgement of a qualified person. Verify every result before installing anything.
