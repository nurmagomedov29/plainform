Reporting a wrong result

If a calculator gives an answer you believe is wrong, that is the most important mail we can receive. Please include:

  • the standard selected, and every input you entered
  • the answer the app gave, and the answer you expected
  • the clause or table you are checking it against, if you have it to hand

A screenshot of the expanded Show the working panel contains almost all of this and is the fastest thing to send.

What these apps are not

They are planning aids. They apply published tables and derating factors to the figures you enter. They do not know your installation, your local amendments, or your inspector. Verify every result against the current edition of the applicable standard before anything is installed.
