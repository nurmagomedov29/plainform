HatchTrack

Privacy, plainly.

Effective 7 September 2026

HatchTrack is an offline-first incubation planner. It does not require an account and does not operate a backend that receives your hatch records.

Information stored on your device

Batch labels, species, egg counts, dates, incubator assignments, hatch results, reminder preferences and reminder-delivery markers are stored locally. You can delete a batch in the app. Uninstalling clears the remaining app data. Android backup and device transfer are disabled.

Advertising and consent

HatchTrack uses Google's User Messaging Platform and Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your region, consent and Google settings, Google may process identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics and app interactions to provide advertising, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. HatchTrack asks for applicable consent before initialising ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Android may display the local batch label and due action. HatchTrack does not use remote push notifications or upload reminder content.

Children

HatchTrack is a husbandry utility for adults and is not designed for children.
