Privacy Policy — HatchTrack

Applies to the Android application app.hatchtrack. Last updated 27 October 2026.

The short version

HatchTrack keeps your hatch records on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no Plainform server, and nothing you enter is uploaded. The free version shows ads from Google; that, and buying Pro, are the only things in the app that use the network.

Information stored on your device

Batch labels, species, egg counts, dates, incubator names, hatch results, reminder preferences and reminder-delivery markers are kept in the app's own private storage, readable by no other app. You can delete a batch in the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it erases everything permanently. Android backup and device transfer are switched off for HatchTrack, so no copy is kept in your Google account.

PDF reports

If you export a batch report as a PDF, the file is created on your phone and shared only when you tap share, to the app you choose. HatchTrack does not send it anywhere itself.

Advertising and consent

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, using Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform. Google may use your device's advertising identifier and other information to serve, measure and protect ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, HatchTrack asks for your consent before any ad is requested.

You can change or withdraw that choice at any time. Where a consent form was shown to you, open Settings in the app and tap Privacy settings; your new choice governs every ad request from then on. Outside those regions there is no recorded choice to revisit, so the control is not shown. Everyone, everywhere, can reset or delete the advertising identifier in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Payment

HatchTrack Pro is a one-time purchase, never a subscription. It unlocks PDF export and removes the ads. It is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: Plainform never sees your payment details or your Google account, and the app receives only a signed confirmation that your Play account owns the purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HatchTrack shows reminders containing the batch label and the action due. They are created on your phone; there are no remote push notifications, and reminder content is never uploaded.

Permissions

Internet and the advertising ID, for ads and purchases only; notifications, for reminders; and run-at-startup, to restore reminders after the phone restarts. HatchTrack requests no location, camera, contacts, photos or storage permission.

Children

HatchTrack is a husbandry tool for adults. It is not designed for or directed at children.
